03Your health information

Your health data is yours. Bloom stores your cycle, symptom and wellbeing entries on your device only. We do not upload them to our servers, and we never sell, rent, or share your health information with advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.

  • Health information is never sold, and never used for advertising or profiling.
  • It is not included in analytics or crash reports.
  • It is stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access, and is encrypted at rest. [Confirm: e.g. SQLCipher / EncryptedSharedPreferences.]

07Children's privacy

Bloom is not directed at children under 13 and is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

08Your rights and data deletion

Because your health data lives on your device, you control it. You can:

  • Delete individual entries inside the App.
  • Erase everything via Settings → Delete all data in the App. [Confirm the menu path.]
  • Uninstall the App, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Bloom → Storage → Clear data. This permanently removes all data.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent. To make a request, email hello@mnvstudio.example. We will respond within 30 days.
