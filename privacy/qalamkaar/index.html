01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how MNV Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the Android app Qalamkaar (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

02Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, and we never ask for your name, email, location or contacts.

Third-party services listed below may collect limited technical data, as described in their own policies.

03How we use information

  • To provide and improve the App's features.
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs.
  • To show advertising that keeps the App free.

We do not sell your personal information.

05Storage and security

Your favourites and reading preferences are stored only on your device.

Data sent to third-party services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we use reasonable safeguards to protect your information.

06Children's privacy

Qalamkaar is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

07Your rights and data deletion

You can delete data stored by the App at any time by clearing its storage (Android Settings → Apps → Qalamkaar → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling it.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent. To make a request, email hello@mnvstudio.example. We will respond within 30 days.

08Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the App.
